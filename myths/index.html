Myth vs Reality

Crowborough Camp: The Facts

The Home Office has announced plans to temporarily house asylum seekers at the Crowborough Army Training Camp. The Home Office’s mishandling of this announcement and lies being spread about asylum seekers by those looking to sow fear and division have led to heightened community tensions.

Crowborough has a proud history of helping people in their hour of need. Refugees fleeing wars in Ukraine and Afghanistan have been welcomed by the community with open arms.

Many local residents are already working to make sure these new arrivals are shown hospitality. Join us in standing up against hateful and racist narratives.

WHAT WE KNOW SO FAR

  • 540 adult male asylum seekers will be temporarily housed
  • They will arrive having been through an initial interview and vetting process where biometric data is taken
  • They receive just £9.95 a week and are not provided mobile phones as standard
  • As they are adults, none will be attending local schools
  • No alcohol is allowed on site
  • The site will be used for 12 months
  • Voices from outside of Crowborough, including far-right and white supremist ones like ‘Carl England’ and Nick Tenconi, are weaponising this issue and brining hate to our streets

“Asylum seekers are a threat to women and girls”

Some people push this message by focusing on shocking individual incidents. However, there is no evidence to back up the claim that asylum seekers, or foreign nationals more generally, pose a disproportionate danger. In fact, the most comprehensive data shows white men are disproportionately likely to commit child sexual assault and that 85% of rapes against women are committed by someone they know.1 2

“Real asylum seekers would be women and children, not single men”

Families fleeing war and persecution are faced with a terrible choice: send the men ahead to find a safe place to rebuild their lives and send for their family later or to undertake the dangerous journey as a family during which women and children are at much higher risk of being targeted. Since 2018, 71% of small boat arrivals have been adult men but just 59% of all asylum seekers are.3 4

“They’re ‘illegals’ and should stay in France”

It is not illegal under international law to enter the country through an irregular route with the intention of claiming asylum. There is also no law the UK is bound by that states you must seek asylum in the first ‘safe’ country you come to.5

“We can’t afford to house them”

The amount the Government spends on supporting asylum seekers is about a tenth of the amount the Government loses to unpaid taxes6. And much of the spending goes to the three main companies housing asylum seekers who have made a combined profit of £383m since the Conservatives contracted them in 2019 7. It’s not asylum seekers who squeeze our services, but rich people who do, by not paying taxes, and companies by taking profits instead of looking after people.

“We’re being overrun already, we can’t take any more”

The UK is home to just 1% of all the refugees in the world - about the same percentage of the world population that British nationals account for. Countries like Turkey, Lebanon and Uganda, who are economically poorer than us, and closer to conflict zones, host many more refugees than the UK does.8 Refugees make up less than 1% of the UK population.8

“They’re not real asylum seekers”

The majority of asylum seekers are successful in proving they are eligible for asylum status.9

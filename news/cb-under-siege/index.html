Crowborough under siege by far right

Feb 22, 2026

Agitators, mobs, and a community terrified to speak out.

CROWBOROUGH is at a breaking point. For four months, weekly protests have taken over the town, growing in power.

The protests have been attended by some of the most dangerous names in British politics.

THE FAR RIGHT AGITATORS

  • Nick Tenconi, who sparked outrage by calling for a “holy war” and branding asylum seekers as “rapists and drug addicts.”
  • Advance UK, who wore “ICE” baseball caps just days after the tragic murder of Renee Good.
  • Tommy Robinson’s stewards and associates like Liam Tuffs, along with extremist Mike Gott (aka Carl England).
  • Raise The Colours
  • AY Audits
  • Christin Konstantin
  • Reform

Instead of being turned away they were met with cheers from the crowd.

THE BETRAYAL

The community feels abandoned by those meant to protect them. Shockingly, Police and Crime Commissioner Katy Bourne has been seen alongside these protesters.

Local MP Nus Ghani has been fueling the fire. Her viral online campaign against those supporting asylum seekers reached GB News and the tabloids.

We believe this “digital harassment” gave a green light to the angry mob that destroyed our event.

VALENTINE’S DAY ATTACK

On Saturday, February 14, we planned a simple, peaceful gesture. We wanted to give out cakes and collect “Welcome Hearts” for those arriving at Crowborough Barracks.

Our peaceful event was hijacked by a hostile, shouting far right-led flash mob. We were outnumbered and feared for our safety. In the end, police had to escort us away.

THE FIGHTBACK

Many people of Crowborough are now living in fear. Many are too scared to stand up or join our cause because of the sheer hostility on our streets.

THIS ENDS NOW. Crowborough Stand Up To Racism is planning a massive UNITY EVENT. We are calling on everyone to join us.

We need numbers. We need strength. We need to show the far right that they do not own our town.

STAND WITH US. STAND AGAINST THE FAR RIGHT.
